On Monday, I enjoyed a relaxing session of listening to Doctor Who audios with Pim. We listened to [Master! Nemesis Express](https://www.bigfinish.com/releases/v/master-nemesis-express-2544), from the worlds of Doctor Who. A horrible evil villain haunts the time vortex (time dimension in Doctor Who), and he has manifested himself in a train traversing the dimension for faster travel speeds. It is up to a plucky bounty hunter and her alien accomplice to stop him, before he regains a permanent body! It's exactly as cheesy as it sounds, which it leans into, making it very entertaining; the taste is like an over the top scifi shooter.

On Wednesday, I was not reading anything in particular. I went to [Tayyiba Biryani](https://maps.app.goo.gl/cKqeuLKoNQPBKUi19) with Yusuf, which was quite nice. After this, I went home, and me and Pim listened to the last episode of Nemesis Express (we hadn't gotten to it on Monday). Having been re-interested in reading due to my conversation with Martin on Tuesday, I then did some arranging of my ebooks. After some hesitating I eventually (on Thursday) got myself the Earthsea series as depicted on the right as an ebook; I read a few chapters and I already love the calm narrative style. It put me in mind of the [Merlin Trilogy by Mary Stewart](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/27689.Mary_Stewart_s_Merlin_Trilogy), which I greatly enjoyed; I bet I'm gonna love Earthsea too.

On Thursday, I read [Board & Conquest, by Maxime Durand](https://www.amazon.nl/Board-Conquest-Kingdom-Building-English-ebook/dp/B0GY1TQZQB/ref=sr_1_1?__mk_nl_NL=%C3%85M%C3%85%C5%BD%C3%95%C3%91&crid=R0N0J4S88RS4&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.BX--1I0xdBN5MLpsVCmTTLFonqfiD2Qadp_XIVf_x1dPHjXWifPjlAhglnN_1EP-yrw6F2Qfag989dNXZ9Z1iv64EDf09XxfiKPK4tiYxkZiXkmLq9WlnGY_C56msZmVcXCgoTcZotknMUzJ0k5f_fMONXawyErQTjDd3QbmXGrVfGXplETpETbQbEqqibFhdc7Z8Sj5c3fmnFq3sHdoYg.PVVGahGtRPmbBQIzNP0M6b1WjDnIl6uvreazLCCuy3s&dib_tag=se&keywords=board+and+conquest&qid=1788703808&sprefix=board+and+conques%2Caps%2C352&sr=8-1). I have read a lot of this author's work. He writes a lot of books in which a protagonist needs to game some RPG-like system, and the author is skilled enough in plot and atmosphere that reading his work can be considered one of my comfort-reads. The plot is never boring and the atmosphere is often well thought out, but you can also guess enough of what is going to happen that the experience does not become taxing. My favourite of this author is still [Vainqueur the Dragon](https://www.amazon.nl/Vainqueur-Dragon-Complete-LitRPG-English-ebook/dp/B0FFLSJ12M/ref=sr_1_2?crid=18OCJMC0RIAA&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.3yX-tE9QTxqfD---mEG1N3gEOtOFxLLGMVko0fPQ11kjfwMvcc3Q6ftwj3uYiDcg4NB8juqpciK6d-rBum9wrA.7OtMy_qqokikb84CxrEoQQCddpwDLPe6AvSSX1xxBgM&dib_tag=se&keywords=vainqueur+the+dragon&qid=1788704073&sprefix=vainqueur+the%2Caps%2C355&sr=8-2), which is one of his sillier works.

Well, in general I tried to make the work office spot a bit less an office spot and a bit more *my* spot, where I can feel comfortable. The second half of the week was a bit more turbulent, especially Thursday. Due to this and due to my increasing headache, Friday was a bit of an off-day for me. but I have a better office spot now (literally and mentally), so if I keep at it I think it'll be fine in the end.
